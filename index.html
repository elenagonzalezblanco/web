Elena González-Blanco is the Head of Artificial Intelligence and Data for Startups and Unicorns across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa at Microsoft. She previously founded Clibrain, a generative AI startup focused on developing language models in Spanish, and served as Global Head of Digital for Wealth Management and Insurance at Banco Santander. She has also held senior leadership roles at Aon, CoverWallet, and Indra. Elena holds a PhD in Philology, has taught and conducted research at Harvard University, and currently teaches at IE Business School, where she is also an Associate Professor. She has led two European ERC research projects on Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, poetry, and music recommendation. She serves as an Independent Board Member and advisor to several companies, speaks five languages, and is the mother of four children.

POSTDATA

Poetry Standardization and Linked Open DataEstandarización de la poesía y datos abiertos enlazados

A European Research Council project (€1.1M, ERC-2015-STG-679528) that developed the first standardized ontological model for European poetry. It created a network of ontologies so that poetry data can be published as Linked Open Data, making centuries of poetic heritage interoperable and machine-readable across languages and traditions.Proyecto del Consejo Europeo de Investigación (1,1 M€, ERC-2015-STG-679528) que desarrolló el primer modelo ontológico estandarizado para la poesía europea. Creó una red de ontologías para publicar los datos poéticos como datos abiertos enlazados, haciendo interoperable y legible por máquinas un patrimonio poético de siglos en distintas lenguas y tradiciones.
